# Edinburgh Stingers Ice Hockey Club — Fee Schedule

This schedule lists current prices for trainings, games, and other billable items and provides a transparent change log. The Board may update this schedule from time to time with reasonable notice to members. Changes to this schedule do not require a constitutional amendment (see Constitution Section 7).

## Policy & process
- Authority: The Board may set or change fees and will give reasonable notice to members before the effective date.
- Recording: Each change records an announced date, effective date, approver, and rationale in the Change Log.
- Enforcement: Payment timing and enforcement are governed by Constitution Section 7.1.
- Hardship: Members may request special arrangements via the Treasurer (Constitution Section 7).

## Current fees (effective 2025-08-31)

| Item              | Price | Notes        |

| Training session  | £18   | Per player   |
| Game              | £20   | Per player   |

Notes
- Visitors and special events may have different rates; any such rates will be communicated in advance for the specific session or game.
- If the rink or governing bodies change costs materially, the Board may adjust fees with reasonable notice; the Change Log below will be updated.
